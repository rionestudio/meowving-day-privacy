Common questions

I lost my progress.
Progress is saved only on your phone — we never receive a copy of it, so we cannot restore it. Deleting the game deletes the save with it.

A level looks impossible.
Every one of the 600 levels has been checked by a solver, so all of them can be cleared. Undo, Restart and the hint button are there for the ones that look stuck.

Can I turn off the sound or vibration?
Yes — Settings in the main menu has separate switches for sound effects, music and vibration.

An ad will not close, or no ad appears.
Ads come from Google and need a connection. If one misbehaves, close the game fully and open it again; nothing in your progress is lost.

How do I change my advertising choices?
In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change that choice later. On iOS, app tracking is controlled in your phone's Settings, under Privacy & Security.
