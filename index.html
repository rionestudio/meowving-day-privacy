Privacy Policy

Meowving Day · last updated 4 October 2026

Meowving Day is a puzzle game made by Rione Studio. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, your email address or your location.

What stays on your device

Your progress — which levels you have cleared, how many moves you used, the stars you earned, the breeds you have met and your sound and vibration settings — is saved only on your phone. We never receive a copy of it. Deleting the game deletes this data with it, and we cannot restore it for you.

Advertising

The game shows ads in two ways. Some you choose to watch in exchange for something — a hint, extra moves, or towing a cart away. Those never start by themselves. Others appear on their own: between levels once you are past level 15, and sometimes when you come back to the game after leaving it. There is a quiet period of several minutes after every ad, so they never arrive one after another.

Ads are delivered by Google AdMob. To show them, Google may collect and use certain information from your device, such as an advertising identifier, your approximate location derived from your IP address and basic details about your device. We do not receive this information ourselves — we only see how many ads were shown in total.

You can read how Google uses this data in the Google Privacy & Terms pages.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change that choice later.
  • On iOS you are asked separately whether the game may track you across other companies' apps. If you say no, you still see ads, they are just less relevant.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your phone's settings, under Privacy.

Children

Meowving Day is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about the game: apps@ralphler-trade.com.

A share of what Meowving Day earns goes to animal shelters. That promise has nothing to do with your data — we mention it here only because people ask whether donations require anything from them. They do not.
